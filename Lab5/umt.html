About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. The university focuses on marine science, aquatic resources, environmental studies and other related fields. It provides higher education opportunities for students who wish to develop their knowledge and skills.

UMT was originally established as a Fisheries and Marine Science Centre under Universiti Pertanian Malaysia in 1979. Over the years, the institution developed and expanded its academic programmes and research activities. It eventually became an independent university known as Universiti Malaysia Terengganu.

Today, UMT offers various academic programmes in science, technology, management and social sciences. The university is committed to producing knowledgeable graduates, conducting quality research and contributing to sustainable development, particularly in marine and coastal environments.
